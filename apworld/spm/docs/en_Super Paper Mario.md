# Super Paper Mario

## What is randomized?
This is a **no-logic prototype**: every location is considered reachable from the start, so items can land anywhere. It exists so players can help work out the real logic.

Depending on your options: the story Pixls, the optional Pixls (Barry, Dashell, Piccolo), key items, items in chests and lying around, and catch cards.

Pure Hearts, characters and the story itself are vanilla. The doors to Chapters 1-7 are all open from the start.

With `skip_cutscenes` (on by default), new files start at Merlon giving you the first Pure Heart, and the Count Bleck scenes between chapters are skipped.

## What is the goal?
Finish Chapter 8.

## What does another world's item look like?
Mario holds up the Archipelago icon, with "Sent <item> to <player>!".
